隐私声明

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 20 日

适用范围与本地数据

本声明适用于 Easel iOS 与 Android 应用。Easel 无账号系统；摆台名称、文字、价格、页面布局、所选图片的应用内副本及设备设置保存在本机，用于编辑和展示摆台。当前版本不向开发者运营的服务器上传这些内容，也不接入广告或分析服务。

应用会在你主动选择图片时读取所选图片并复制到应用存储区；不会为创建摆台而遍历整个相册。应用使用系统文件选择器读取你主动选择的交换文件。

存储、分享与第三方

摆台记录保存在设备应用空间，直到你删除相关摆台或通过系统删除应用数据。删除摆台后，未被其他摆台引用的图片副本会在应用下次启动时清理；导入和导出的临时交换文件可能留在应用缓存，直到系统清理或你清除应用数据。设备操作系统的备份功能也可能另行保存副本，是否备份及删除由你在系统或备份服务中管理。

只有你主动点击“分享”，Easel 才会生成包含摆台内容及相关图片的交换文件，并交给系统分享面板。文件可能包含你填入的个人信息；接收人、分享渠道及渠道后续处理由你选择和控制。请在分享前检查内容，不要通过不可信渠道传送敏感信息。

权限、删除与权利

相册或文件访问仅用于你主动选图、导入或分享时的相应操作。你可以在系统设置中撤销权限；撤销后相关功能可能无法继续使用，但已保存的摆台不会自动删除。

你可在“我的摆台”删除单个摆台，也可通过设备系统卸载应用或清除应用数据。删除应用内数据不会删除你已发给他人、存入其他应用或留在设备备份中的交换文件。当前版本没有开发者服务器上的个人资料可供远程删除。

安全、变更与联系

Easel 不会把交换文件加密；请妥善保护设备和分享文件。我们会在数据处理方式发生实质变化时更新本声明，并在应用内提供可查看的最新版本。

隐私问题、权利请求或安全问题请联系“关于”页列出的开发者邮箱。由于当前内容主要存储在你的设备上，开发者通常无法直接读取、修改或找回本地摆台。

Privacy Notice

Effective September 20, 2026

Scope and local data

This notice applies to the Easel iOS and Android apps. Easel has no accounts. Booth names, text, prices, page layouts, app-local copies of selected images, and device settings are stored on your device to edit and display booths. The current version does not upload this content to a developer-operated server or use advertising or analytics services.

When you choose an image, the app reads that selected image and stores a copy in its app space; it does not scan your entire photo library to create a booth. A system document picker reads only exchange files you choose to import.

Storage, sharing, and third parties

Booth records stay in app storage until you delete the booth or remove app data through the operating system. Image copies no longer referenced by any booth are cleaned up the next time the app starts. Temporary imported and exported exchange files may remain in the app cache until the OS clears it or you remove app data. Your operating system may keep separate backups; manage and delete those copies through the OS or backup provider.

Only when you tap Share does Easel create an exchange file containing the booth and its images and hand it to the system share sheet. It may contain personal information you entered. You choose the recipient and channel; their subsequent handling is outside Easel. Review the file before sharing and avoid untrusted channels for sensitive content.

Permissions, deletion, and choices

Photo and file access is used for actions you initiate: selecting images, importing, or sharing. You can revoke permissions in system settings. Related features may then stop working, but saved booths are not automatically deleted.

You can delete individual booths in My Booths or remove app data through your device settings or by uninstalling. This does not erase exchange files already sent to others, saved in other apps, or held in device backups. This version has no developer-hosted user profile to delete remotely.

Security, changes, and contact

Easel does not encrypt exchange files. Protect your device and shared files. We will update this notice if our data practices materially change and make the current version available in the app.

For privacy questions, rights requests, or security concerns, use the developer email on the About screen. Because the current version stores content mainly on your device, the developer generally cannot access, edit, or recover your local booths.
